# Colourable Aquariums - Steam Workshop v1.0

Give your aquariums a custom colour! Select an aquarium room and click Choose Colour to use the colour wheel and swatches. Water, glass and aquarium lighting follow your chosen colour. Dredge tanks have a softer glow at 25% of normal aquarium brightness, and colours stay matched when Dredge fish are added or removed.

Colours are saved per museum. Reset restores the original appearance.

## Required: TPM Mod Loader

Install TPM Mod Loader v1.2.5 or later in your Two Point Museum game folder. Subscribing to a DLL mod alone does not install the loader. Open the loader download location and copy its installation files into the game folder following the loader instructions.

With the compatible loader installed, subscribe here, let Steam finish downloading, and restart the game. This mod's DLL can remain in the Workshop download folder; no item-ID configuration is needed.

## Optional: Expanded Colour Picker

Adds expanded swatches, RGB/HEX colour entry and an eyedropper. Colourable Aquariums also works on its own.

## Existing local users

Local/Nexus installation remains supported. Remove an older local `Mods/TPM-AquaColour.dll` if you want to use the subscribed version, because the loader gives a successful local copy priority. Preserve your mod's saved colour data when updating or moving installation locations.

Windows x64 only. Restart after updates. Game updates may require a mod compatibility update.
